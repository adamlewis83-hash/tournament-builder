## Description (updated opening; rest of the existing description stands)

Sporos runs any tournament — pickleball, golf, disc golf, tennis, cornhole,
cup pong, or your own made-up game — from the first bracket to the final
trophy.

SEED. PLAY. CROWN.
• Pick a sport and a format: single or double elimination, round robin, Swiss,
  pools-to-bracket, King of the Court, Americano/Mexicano, Ryder Cup, or full
  golf scoring.
• Add players or teams — or let them register themselves with a QR code — and
  Sporos builds the schedule or bracket instantly.
• Score live from any phone. Spectators follow along with a join code and cheer
  from the feed. No accounts, no ads, no trackers.
• Golf gets the full treatment: stroke play (gross/net), Stableford, skins with
  carryovers, Nassau, handicaps applied hole by hole, and GPS distance to the
  pin on a satellite map.
• Every champion lands in the Record Book.

Free to use. An optional email sign-in backs up your tournaments across
devices — no password, nothing to remember. Delete your account (and all cloud
data) any time, right in Settings.
