# Instagram launch kit

## Before you post (5 min)

1. **Bio link:** set your profile link to https://sporos.app (it forwards people
   to the App Store badge + works for Android friends via the web app). Or link
   the App Store directly: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sporos-tournament-builder/id6787539978
2. **Record with the demo account** so fictional names are on screen: in the
   app, Settings → Account & sync → sign out, then sign in as
   adamlewis83+demo@gmail.com (code arrives in your Gmail). Sign back into your
   real account after filming.
3. iPhone screen recording: Control Center → screen record. Portrait. Do a
   slow, deliberate run-through — you can trim/speed up in the IG editor or
   CapCut.

## Shot list (~30 seconds total)

| Time | Shot | What to show |
|------|------|--------------|
| 0–4s | Home | The three tournament cards (LIVE/FINAL pills visible). Slow scroll. |
| 4–10s | New tournament | Tap +, tap Pickleball, tap a format tile, Create. Show how fast setup is. |
| 10–16s | Seed it | Player list filled → tap "Seed it → generate bracket" → bracket pops in. |
| 16–22s | Live scoring | Cup Pong Night: the "On court now" card, tap + a few times, LEADING badge. |
| 22–28s | The crown | Sunset Open: champion screen — "Sam Rivera & Riley Chen is crowned", confetti, podium. |
| 28–30s | End | Home screen or the App Store page. (Text overlay: "Sporos — free on the App Store") |

Tip: film each segment separately and cut them — snappier than one take.

## Caption — option A (the story, recommended)

Little life update: I built an app. 🌱🏆

It started because our Saturday pickleball group could never keep the bracket
straight — someone always ran it off a whiteboard photo that was wrong by
round two. So I spent my nights building Sporos: pick a sport, pick a format,
and it builds the bracket or schedule in seconds. Everyone scores from their
own phone, friends can follow along live, and somebody gets *crowned* at the
end.

Pickleball, golf (with real handicaps and skins), cornhole, cup pong, tennis,
board games — basically anything you can compete at.

It's free, no ads, and as of this week it's officially on the App Store,
which still feels fake to type. Link in bio. If you try it, run your next
game night on it and tell me what breaks — and if you like it, a rating
genuinely helps a tiny app get found. 🙏

Seed. Play. Crown.

## Story (post right after the feed post)

- Slide 1: repost the feed video, text: "I made a thing 🌱 → link below",
  add the **link sticker** → https://sporos.app (stickers are tappable;
  captions aren't).
- Slide 2 (optional): screenshot of the ⭐ 5.0 App Store rating, text:
  "first rating in 🥹 — run your game night on it, link in last slide".

## After posting

- Reply to every comment (comments drive reach more than likes).
- Pin the post to your profile.
- ~1 week later, one follow-up Story with a real milestone ("14 tournaments
  run this week 👀") — milestones age better than reminders.
- Don't repost the same ask; next feed post should be a real event (photo of
  the crew + "crowned a champion with Sporos" energy).
